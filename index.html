How It Works

From brief to final file in days

Fill out the contact form with your project goal, the type of video you need, and your deadline. No need to have everything figured out. That is what the next step is for.

Once we agree on scope, you pay a 50% deposit to lock in your slot. I then send you a structured intake form covering your brand assets, style references, inspiration videos, and key message. If you have a script ready, include it. If not, we write it together.

For SaaS and Reels, I write the script and map the visual direction before a single frame is touched. You review and approve. For Talking Head, we move straight to production from your intake.

I animate and edit your video end-to-end. Motion graphics, color grading, BGM, SFX, and captions all handled. You do not manage separate pieces. You get a complete first draft.

You review the draft and send all your feedback in one go. I apply every change in one revision round. Once approved, you pay the remaining 50% and receive the final MP4 formatted for your platform, ready to upload.
