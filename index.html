About Me

I'm Clive James B. Dominise, a multi-disciplinary creative designer specializing in UI design, motion graphics, video editing, and poster design. I believe that design is not just about making things look good — it's about telling stories and creating experiences that resonate with people.

My journey started with mobile editing apps, and today I'm proficient in Alight Motion, CapCut, and Canva. Currently, I'm expanding my skillset by learning Adobe Photoshop, After Effects, and Figma to bring more professional and high-quality outputs to my work.
